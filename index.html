Word puzzle · Android

Wildebeest Crossing

Arcade game · Phone, Android TV, PC

Cross the Mara. Dodge the crocs.

Guide a brave wildebeest across the Mara River. Steer with a joystick, tilt or your TV remote, eat the grass and reach the flag. Endless levels, a Daily Crossing, and 1v1 races against friends.

Family care · Android

Small steps. Big love.

A companion for parents, teachers and therapists of autistic children. Plan daily routines, log moments in seconds, open a Calm Mode that works with no signal, and share clear reports with a child's doctor or school.
