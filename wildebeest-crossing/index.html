Wildebeest Crossing

Tilt your phone to guide a brave wildebeest across the Mara River. Eat the grass, dodge the crocodiles and reach the flag. The migration never ends.

Become a tester

Play the game on Android before it's released. It's free and takes about two minutes.

You need: an Android phone and the Google account you use in the Play Store. Use that same account for every step.

  1. Join the testers group.
    • Tap the button below and sign in with your Google account if asked.
    • Tap Join group, then Join group again to confirm. You can choose "No email" if you don't want messages.
    • You're in when the button changes to Leave group.
    1. Join the testers group
  2. Accept the test invite on Google Play.
    • Tap the button below, signed in with the same account.
    • Tap Become a tester. The page then says you are a tester.
    • If it says the app isn't available, wait a few minutes after joining the group and try again.
    2. Become a tester
  3. Download the game.
    • Tap the button below to open Wildebeest Crossing in the Play Store.
    • Tap Install, then Play.
    3. Download on Google Play
  4. Keep it installed and play. Please keep the game on your phone for at least 14 days. Google only lets it go public once enough testers have done that.
Something not working?
  • "App not available" or "Item not found": the Play Store is using a different Google account from the one that joined the group. Open the Play Store, tap your profile picture and switch to the right account.
  • The invite page won't accept you: check that step 1 shows Leave group, wait a few minutes and reload.
  • Still stuck, or found a bug? Email me and I'll help.
