Wildebeest Crossing: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Wildebeest Crossing ("the game") is made by Owori Juma ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. We have no user accounts and no servers of our own, and we do not sell personal information.

Information stored only on your device

Your progress (unlocked crossings, stars and best times), coins, coats, wakes, daily-crossing streak and settings are saved on your phone. We never receive them. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location is worked out,
  • information about your device and how you interact with ads,
  • diagnostic and crash information about the ads.

Google uses this information under its own policies. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. On Android you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device's Google settings.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Purchases such as Remove Ads, the Supporter Pack and coin packs are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google tells the game which items you own, so they can be restored after a reinstall.

Leaderboards (Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, your scores (total stars, furthest crossing and daily crossing score) are sent to Google Play Games leaderboards, together with your Play Games player name and ID. Google handles this under the Google Privacy Policy. You can sign out or change your Play Games profile visibility in the Play Games app.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

Information sent by the Google services above is encrypted in transit.

Changes

If the game starts using new services, such as cloud save or analytics, we will update this policy and the date above.
