Wildebeest Crossing: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Wildebeest Crossing ("the game") is made by Owori Juma ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. You can play without an account. An anonymous online account is created only if you open VS (1v1 races) or Leaderboards, or allow notifications. We do not sell personal information.

Information stored only on your device

Your progress (unlocked crossings, stars and best times), coins, coats, wakes, daily-crossing streak and settings are saved on your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes them. If you have an online account (below), a copy of your progress and coin balance is kept with it so you can get them back on a new install, and the leaderboard numbers described below are sent to us.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location is worked out,
  • information about your device and how you interact with ads,
  • diagnostic and crash information about the ads.

Google uses this information under its own policies. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. On Android you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device's Google settings. On iPhone and iPad the game asks for permission before it uses your device's advertising identifier, and you can change that choice in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Purchases (Google Play and the App Store)

Purchases such as Remove Ads, the Supporter Pack and coin packs are handled by Google Play on Android and by Apple's App Store on iPhone and iPad. We never see your payment details. The store tells the game which items you own, so they can be restored after a reinstall. To stop fake or reused receipts, the game's server checks each purchase with Google Play or Apple and keeps a record of it (the item, the store's order or transaction number, the time, and your game account ID). If a purchase is refunded, the coins or item it gave are taken back.

1v1 races and your VS wallet (Google Firebase)

If you open VS, the game signs you in anonymously with Google Firebase Authentication. No name, email or phone number is asked for. Firebase gives you a random account ID, and the game stores the following with it on Google Cloud servers (Firebase, region europe-west1):

  • your racing name, shown to your rival: one you choose, or a generated one such as "Gnu #4F2A", and the coat you wear,
  • reports other players make about a racing name, used only to remove offensive names,
  • your VS wallet balance, the coins you move in or out, and a record of your stakes and payouts,
  • your races: who you raced, the stake, the result, your time, grass eaten and hearts left,
  • while a race is running, your wildebeest's position on the river, sent to your rival a few times a second and deleted about ten minutes after the race,
  • friend codes you create, which expire after 24 hours.

Racing names are checked for offensive words. A name that several players report is removed, and its owner goes back to a generated name. Coins have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money. We use this information only to run races, settle stakes fairly and prevent cheating.

Teams and leaderboards (Google Firebase)

If you open Leaderboards, the game uses the same anonymous online account as VS. It stores with it, on the same Google Cloud servers:

  • your leaderboard numbers: total stars, furthest crossing, your best Daily Crossing score for each day (and the totals for the week and month), and how many 1v1 races you have won,
  • the country league you race for: guessed from your phone's region setting (not your location) and changeable in Settings,
  • the teams you create or join, your role in them (owner, admin or member), and the names of teams you create,
  • reports other players make about a team name, used only to remove offensive names,
  • prizes you win at the end of a week or season, and whether you have collected them.

Other players see your racing name, coat and leaderboard numbers on the boards of the teams and country league you are in. Anyone can find a public team by name and see its board; private teams are joined with a code. Team names are checked for offensive words, and a name that several players report is removed. Team owners and admins can remove players from their team.

If you are signed in to Game Center (iPhone and iPad) or Google Play Games (Android), the game links your online account to it, so your VS wallet, teams and scores come back when you install the game on a new device. We receive only an ID for that account from Apple or Google, not your email or password.

Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

If you allow notifications, the game stores a Firebase installation ID for your device with your online account, your time zone, and the day you last crossed the Daily Crossing and your streak. We use them to tell you when a rival is found, a friend takes your code or a race is settled, to remind you about the Daily Crossing in the evening, to tell you when someone passes you on a team board or when you win a week or a prize, and to send occasional news about seasons and the game. You can turn each kind off in Settings → Notifications, or turn notifications off for the game in your phone's settings. On iPhone and iPad, notifications are delivered through the Apple Push Notification service.

Deleting your online data

In Settings, Delete online account deletes your anonymous account and the information listed above that is linked to it, including any coins left in your VS wallet. It also takes you out of every team (a team you run passes to another member, and a team with no one left is deleted) and removes you from every board. Uninstalling the game does not delete it, so use that button first, or email us at the address below and we will delete it.

Leaderboards (Google Play Games and Game Center)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, your scores (total stars, furthest crossing and daily crossing score) are sent to Google Play Games leaderboards, together with your Play Games player name and ID. Google handles this under the Google Privacy Policy. You can sign out or change your Play Games profile visibility in the Play Games app.

On iPhone and iPad, if you are signed in to Game Center, the same scores are sent to Apple's Game Center leaderboards with your Game Center nickname. Apple handles this under the Apple Privacy Policy. You can sign out or change your profile in Settings → Game Center.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

Information sent by the Google and Apple services above is encrypted in transit.

Changes

If the game starts using new services, such as cloud save or analytics, we will update this policy and the date above.
