Wildebeest Crossing: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Wildebeest Crossing ("the game") is made by Owori Juma ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. You can play without an account. An anonymous online account is created only if you open VS (1v1 races) or allow notifications. We do not sell personal information.

Information stored only on your device

Your progress (unlocked crossings, stars and best times), coins, coats, wakes, daily-crossing streak and settings are saved on your phone. We never receive them. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location is worked out,
  • information about your device and how you interact with ads,
  • diagnostic and crash information about the ads.

Google uses this information under its own policies. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. On Android you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device's Google settings.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Purchases such as Remove Ads, the Supporter Pack and coin packs are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google tells the game which items you own, so they can be restored after a reinstall.

1v1 races and your VS wallet (Google Firebase)

If you open VS, the game signs you in anonymously with Google Firebase Authentication. No name, email or phone number is asked for. Firebase gives you a random account ID, and the game stores the following with it on Google Cloud servers (Firebase, region europe-west1):

  • your racing name, shown to your rival: one you choose, or a generated one such as "Gnu #4F2A", and the coat you wear,
  • reports other players make about a racing name, used only to remove offensive names,
  • your VS wallet balance, the coins you move in or out, and a record of your stakes and payouts,
  • your races: who you raced, the stake, the result, your time, grass eaten and hearts left,
  • while a race is running, your wildebeest's position on the river, sent to your rival a few times a second and deleted about ten minutes after the race,
  • friend codes you create, which expire after 24 hours.

Racing names are checked for offensive words. A name that several players report is removed, and its owner goes back to a generated name. Coins have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money. We use this information only to run races, settle stakes fairly and prevent cheating.

Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

If you allow notifications, the game stores a Firebase installation ID for your device with your online account, your time zone, and the day you last crossed the Daily Crossing and your streak. We use them to tell you when a rival is found, a friend takes your code or a race is settled, to remind you about the Daily Crossing in the evening, and to send occasional news about seasons and the game. You can turn each kind off in Settings → Notifications, or turn notifications off for the game in your phone's settings.

Deleting your online data

In Settings, Delete online account deletes your anonymous account and the information listed above that is linked to it, including any coins left in your VS wallet. Uninstalling the game does not delete it, so use that button first, or email us at the address below and we will delete it.

Leaderboards (Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, your scores (total stars, furthest crossing and daily crossing score) are sent to Google Play Games leaderboards, together with your Play Games player name and ID. Google handles this under the Google Privacy Policy. You can sign out or change your Play Games profile visibility in the Play Games app.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Security

Information sent by the Google services above is encrypted in transit.

Changes

If the game starts using new services, such as cloud save or analytics, we will update this policy and the date above.
